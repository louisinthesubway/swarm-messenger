## What SWARM changed, and what it did not

SWARM Messenger is a modified version of Signal Desktop. The modifications are
listed, with reasons and file paths, in
[`docs/SWARM-CHANGES.md`](docs/SWARM-CHANGES.md). In summary:

- branding, product name, icons, colours, typography and user-visible strings
- every server endpoint, so that the app talks only to SWARM servers and **never**
  to Signal's
- phone-number registration made reachable as a first device
- donations, badges, update checks and Signal's support and community links removed
- a placeholder for the SWARM wallet in the left navigation

**Nothing cryptographic was changed.** The protocol library is upstream
`@signalapp/libsignal-client` from npm, unmodified, including its post-quantum key
agreement (PQXDH) and post-quantum ratchet (SPQR). No primitive, no key schedule
and no wire format was touched.
